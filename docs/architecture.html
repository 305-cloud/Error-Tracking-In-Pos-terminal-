nairatrace-v2 · architecture

How NairaTrace works

NairaTrace simulates Nigerian payments hop by hop, breaks entities on purpose, and records two separate things: what the payment system would log, and what really caused each failure. A model then has to recover the second from the first.

The pipeline

The four routes

Each route is a chain of hops. Every hop can stop the payment, and each one answers with its own codes. These are the codes a hop sends back when it fails.

How a customer ends up debited for a failed payment

Every hop waits for the next one with its own timeout. When a slow issuer answers after the scheme has given up, the money has already left the account while the terminal says "failed". This one mechanism produces most of the hard cases.

Where each piece lives

FileWhat it does
params/default.yamlEvery number in the model, each with its source: a citation, or a tag for estimate [E], snippet-level source [S] or company claim [C].
world.pyBuilds the population: customers with banks, cards, KYC tiers and balances; agents tied to one principal, one terminal and one PTSA; and every entity.
entities.pyEach PTSA, switch, scheme, bank channel, gateway and principal flips between UP, DEGRADED and DOWN (failures more likely at month-end and in December), and slows down as it gets busy.
routes.pyThe four routes as SimPy processes: hop-by-hop calls with timeouts, issuer and bank decisions, CBN caps and geo-fence, reversals and status queries.
codes.pyCard DE39, NIP and local code tables. Each code has one owner and is marked business or technical, and approved, declined or unknown.
trace.pyWrites observable spans and transactions; keeps the true cause in a separate table.
sim.pyHourly arrivals and daily income, runs the clock, then writes the Parquet tables and a provenance file.
validate.pyCompares a run with NIBSS and CBN figures: volume mix, failure rates, ticket size, burstiness, refund timing.
localise.pyBuilds features from observable logs only, then scores the rule and the gradient-boosting model on a different seed.
scenarios.pyThe stress scenario: more frequent, more often slow faults, so every role fails enough to train on.
